Graph vs dense retrieval probe result (2026-08-18)

Harness: benchmarks/ab_graph_retrieval_or.py, 2 LoCoMo conversations ingested
WITH entity extraction (anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5 via OpenRouter), LOCAL
all-MiniLM embedder, retrieval-only hit_rate against gold evidence dia_ids,
top_k=30, no responder/judge.

category        n   dense   graph   delta  fired
multi-hop      43   0.281   0.297  +0.016     35
open-domain    11   0.439   0.439  +0.000     11
single-hop    114   0.395   0.404  +0.009     84
temporal       63   0.698   0.730  +0.032     46
ALL           231   0.459   0.474  +0.016

Cost of graph-mode ingest, measured on the same run:
- conv-26: 419 turns -> 4,230 records (10.1x amplification), ~2.9 CPU-hours
  of graph building on one core plus ~1 extraction call per turn.
- conv-30: 369 turns -> 3,444 records (9.3x).
- Dense-mode ingest of the same turns is ~10 ms/turn with no LLM calls.

READ (honest null-to-marginal): the rebuilt graph search is ACTIVE (returned a
different result set than dense on 176/231 questions) and never regresses, but
its gains are marginal (+0.016 aggregate, +0.016 on multi-hop, its target
category; the largest gain is temporal +0.032). Weighed against a multi-
thousand-fold ingest cost increase and 10x storage amplification, graph mode
does not earn its cost on LoCoMo evidence retrieval. This closes the question
paper 1 (Sec 5.6) left open with "the burden of proof sits with graph": on
this evidence, dense remains the validated default.

Caveats: 2 conversations, local embedder, retrieval-only (no answer accuracy);
absolute hit rates are not comparable to the published retrieval_quality.py
numbers (different embedder and scoring scope). Within-run paired deltas are
the finding.

Reproduce: OPENROUTER_API_KEY=... python benchmarks/ab_graph_retrieval_or.py --n-convs 2
