Note the direction honestly: Mem0's point estimate is nominally HIGHER here,
as it was in the published in-window LoCoMo table (0.855 vs 0.851). The claim
was, and remains, a tie on answer accuracy. GENOME's advantages are elsewhere:
0 LLM calls per message at ingest, air-gapped operation, and a deterministic
auditable record.

This is the fifth-plus independent configuration in which GENOME-vs-Mem0
answer accuracy comes back at parity (LoCoMo n=1540 p=0.747; LongMemEval n=90
p=0.19; LongMemEval n=205 p=0.14; bge-small n=90 p=0.83; bge-large n=90 p=0.82;
this locked rerun n=300 p=0.382). No configuration has produced a significant
difference in either direction.

Cost of this run: roughly $20 of metered OpenRouter spend, about 4 CPU-hours,
dominated by Mem0's LLM ingestion (727-1546 s per conversation) versus GENOME's
local embedding write path.
