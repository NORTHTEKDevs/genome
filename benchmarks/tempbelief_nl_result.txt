Caveats, stated plainly:
- Backend differs from the published explicit-register run (OpenRouter + local
  embedder here vs direct Anthropic + OpenAI embedder there): within-harness
  comparisons are valid; cross-run absolute numbers are indicative only.
- Mem0's current-value dropped to 0.500 here vs 0.861 in the explicit-register
  run. Whether that is the register or the local-embedder backend is
  UNRESOLVED; do not cite Mem0's absolute numbers without this caveat.
- Single run, single seed, template-authored dataset (self-built; generator and
  canonical JSON are committed - regeneration needs PYTHONHASHSEED=0).
